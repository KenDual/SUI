# Intensifier

## Project Overview

**Intensifier** is a project aimed at building a **non-traditional credit scoring tool** that supports and enhances the way businesses evaluate creditworthiness. It is designed to address real-world challenges that enterprises face when assessing potential borrowers, especially those who fall outside traditional credit evaluation methods.

In the **traditional financial system**, a customer’s credit score is typically determined through transactional records and historical loan data stored within credit bureaus. This approach remains widely used by banks in Vietnam and many other markets.

However, these traditional criteria have **inherent limitations**. They can be subjective and often fail to capture the true financial behavior of certain customer groups — particularly **credit-worthy individuals who lack formal credit histories**. For example:

* A recent graduate with a stable job may struggle to secure their first loan due to the absence of any credit history.
* Small business owners and market vendors in rural areas often operate primarily with cash and have limited use of formal banking services. Although they may be financially responsible and capable of repaying loans, their lack of formal credit records makes it difficult for traditional systems to evaluate their risk accurately.

**Intensifier** seeks to expand the perspective on credit evaluation by incorporating unconventional data sources, machine learning models, and alternative behavioral signals to generate more inclusive and equitable credit scores.

## Key Goals

* Develop a **non-traditional credit scoring model** that goes beyond historical loan data.
* Provide businesses with tools to **better assess credit risk**, especially for under-served customer segments.
* Enhance financial inclusion by enabling access to credit for individuals with limited or no formal credit history.
* Offer a flexible framework that can be adapted or extended for various industries and use cases.

## Why This Matters

Traditional credit scoring systems often fail to recognize the true financial capacity of individuals who do not fit conventional profiles. **Intensifier** tackles this gap by leveraging alternative signals and intelligent modeling to create a more holistic view of credit behavior — enabling fairer, data-driven decisions that benefit both lenders and borrowers.
